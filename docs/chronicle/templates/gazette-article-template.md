# Goblin Gazette article — template for ChatGPT

Give ChatGPT this whole file, plus the campaign facts it may use. Ask for **seven articles** at a time,
each in exactly the format below. Nothing here is canon until the DM approves it.

Rules for the writer:
- Write only what the **players could know**. No DM-only secrets, no unrevealed plot.
- Do not invent campaign events that have not happened at the table. Rumors, gossip, opinion and
  advertisements are fine if they are labelled as such (see "Kind of reporting").
- Keep the Gazette's voice: the reporters below each have their own voice.
- Link Chronicle pages with `[[page-address]]` or `[[page-address|shown text]]` only for pages that exist.

Reporters: `editor` (Gristlewick Snoot, grand and mistaken), `nib` (Nib Twelve-Fingers, suspicious lists),
`mossbottom` (Mossbottom Quill, plain and frightened war correspondent), `pinchcheek` (Madame Pinchcheek,
warm and nosy gossip), `corrections` (The Corrections Desk, grudging).

Sections: `breaking`, `war`, `local`, `rumors`, `interviews`, `corrections`.
Kind of reporting: `confirmed` (reported), `rumor` (unconfirmed gossip), `satire` (editorial/opinion), `correction`.
