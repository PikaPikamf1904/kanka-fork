# Letters from Krynn — template for ChatGPT

Give ChatGPT this whole file, plus the campaign facts it may use. Ask for a few letters at a time,
each in exactly the format below. Nothing here is canon until the DM approves it.

Rules for the writer:
- A letter is one piece of correspondence, not a conversation. No replies, no back-and-forth.
- Write only what the **party could know**. No DM-only secrets.
- Do not invent campaign events that have not happened at the table. Thanks, requests, complaints,
  invitations and approved rumors are fine when they refer only to things the DM confirms.
- Sound like the sender, not like a narrator. Keep it short: 80–250 words.
