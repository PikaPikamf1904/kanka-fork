Specific conflicts to settle:

1. **Charlie vs Charlie Jennings.** The brief lists "Charlie Jennings" and separately says
   "Charlie" was captured. They are treated as one person on one page; confirm.
2. **Captured, not convicted.** Nothing says Charlie committed the murder. The pages say only
   "captured".
3. **Six named figures with no role** (Captain Mac, Serafina, Nicholas, Inga, George Evans,
   William Mournhill). They are listed as "known or proposed"; whether each has appeared in play,
   and in which adventure, is unknown. They are not linked to the Bone Pelican.
4. **Current base.** "Kalaman / current operating region" and "the current port" are both
   mentioned; which is right is unknown.
5. **Who is a player character.** Swift Claw, Frost, Orkus, Pubert and Gears are listed with the
   party as "membership unverified".
6. **Spellings.** The brief warns that names differ across sources; the pack uses the brief's.
