# Chronicle: player guide

Status: this describes the Chronicle features on the development branch. They are **not deployed**.

This guide is for players. Your Dungeon Master (DM) sets up your access. Everything here is about the site you use during the campaign.

## Joining with your invitation link

Your DM sends you a personal link. It looks like a normal web address that ends in a long code after a `#`.

1. Open the whole link on your phone or computer.
2. The page says "Welcome, <your name>". Check that it is your name.
3. Choose **I'm <your name>, let me in**.

There is no email address and no password. The link works once. After you have used it, opening it again shows "This link didn't work" with a short general message. If it has expired, or your DM has replaced or cancelled it, you see the same screen. Ask your DM for a new one.

Other things to know:

- The page removes the code from the address bar as soon as it opens, so it is not left in the address bar.
- The device stays signed in until you choose **Leave on this device** or your DM signs that device out.
- **Another device** (for example, a phone as well as a laptop): ask your DM for a *link for another device*. It adds that device to your same account, so your journal and notes are the same on both.
- **A shared device** (a family tablet, for example): after you join, the site remembers that device for you. If other players have also joined on the same device, the menu shows **Switch to <name>**, and the start screen shows **Continue as <name>**. You can only switch to a player who joined on that device. Switching does not need a code.
- **Leave on this device** signs out only this device. Your other devices stay signed in. To come back to this device later, you need a new invitation link from your DM.
- Access codes from your DM still work. A device that enters an access code is its own player.

## Saved pages and reading lists

Open **Saved pages** from the menu or the command palette.

- **Save**: a Save button sits near the title of every page. Choose it to keep the page in your saved pages. A small menu next to it lets you put the page in a reading list. Choosing **Saved** again removes the page from your saved pages and from every list.
- **Reading lists**: make lists to sort your saved pages (for example, "Harbour" or "Session 12 prep"). You can rename, delete and reorder lists. You can move a saved page to another list, or back to plain saved pages, with the **Move to** menu.
- Deleting a reading list does not delete its pages from the wiki. It removes them from your saved pages.
- You can only save a page you can open. A page that is hidden later stops showing in your list.

**Recently viewed** is a short list of the pages you opened lately. It is kept **in this browser only**, not in your account. It does not follow you to another device. Use **Clear recently viewed** to remove it.

## My scratchpad

A scratchpad is a private note.

- Each wiki page has a **My scratchpad** panel. Open it to write a note about that page.
- The general scratchpad (not tied to one page) is at **/scratchpad**, titled "My scratchpad".
- Notes save by themselves a moment after you stop typing. You can also choose **Save note**.
- Only you can see your scratchpad. The DM cannot read it.
- If you edit the same note on two devices, the site will not overwrite a newer version. It asks you to copy your text, reload and try again.

## Your journal

Open **Journal** from the menu. The journal is for your character's theories, goals, suspicions and questions as the story goes on.

- **New entry** starts an entry. Give it a title, a date and any text. The text can use Markdown, and `[[Page name]]` links to wiki pages.
- **Tags**: choose up to five tags for each entry. The tags are **Theory**, **Goal**, **Suspicion**, **Question** and **Note**.
- **Linked pages**: search for wiki pages in the entry and link them. Linked pages appear as chips under the entry. A linked page that is hidden or removed is left out of the list.
- **Search your journal** finds words in titles and text. The tag buttons above the entries filter by tag.
- The entries are shown as a **timeline**, grouped by month, newest first.
- **Edit** changes an entry. **Delete** removes it after a confirmation. A deleted entry cannot be recovered.
- If you change an entry on two devices, the site asks you to copy your text, reload and try again instead of overwriting the newer version.

## Feedback for the DM

Open **Feedback for the DM** from the menu or the command palette. Use it to tell your DM what is working, what isn't, and what you would like to change, about the campaign or about this website.

1. Choose what it is about. The first choice is **Boundaries or discomfort**. Use it for anything that made you uncomfortable, or any line you would rather not cross. You do not need to explain or justify it. A few words are enough.
2. Write your message (up to 5,000 characters).
3. Choose how to send it:
   - **With my name**: the DM knows it is from you and can reply on this page. You can see your signed feedback and the DM's reply here, under **Feedback you signed**.
   - **Anonymously**: no name, and no reply is possible. Anonymous feedback is not listed back to you.
4. Choose **Send to the DM**.

Two rules apply to all feedback: only the DM reads it, and it never appears anywhere else on the site. For the details of what is stored, see `FEEDBACK-PRIVACY.md`.

The anonymity note on the form says this, word for word:

> Chronicle does not attach your name or account and does not store who sent this or the time (only the date). Please know the limits: your writing style or the details you mention may still give you away, and the hosting providers keep ordinary request logs, so complete anonymity cannot be promised.

If you are not sure whether anonymity matters for what you want to say, you can always choose **With my name** and talk to the DM directly.

## Knowledge Studio (research collections)

Open **Knowledge Studio** in the menu (`/studio`).

- **Collections:** create one per question you are researching ("Who funds the cult?"). A collection is yours alone unless you tick
  **Share with the party** under *About this collection*; the rest of the party (and the DM) can then read it but not change it.
- **Sources:** add pages by name. Only pages you can read can be added. Give each a **topic**, a **quote from the page** and **your note**.
  Every quote shows which page it came from.
- **Research summaries:** write what you have worked out and cite sources by their number, like `[1]` or `[2, 3]`.
- **Search this collection**, **Copy source list**, and **Export (Markdown)** to keep a copy.
- **Related pages** lists pages connected to your sources that are not in the collection yet.
- If a page is hidden later, it appears as "A source that is no longer available" and is never named.

No AI is used. Available on the development branch; not deployed.
